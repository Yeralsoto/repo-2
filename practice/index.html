Some things I learn because they are useful.

Others become useful later.

The Practice · 10 studies · 2026

Underwriting

I do not only underwrite land.

I underwrite what has to become true for a project to work.

Land.Subdivisions.Multifamily.Development projects.Capital improvements.Existing assets.Ground-up.Value-add.

What is absorbing? What is slowing?

What survives after the assumptions move?

Multifamily

Managing buildings taught me to read one four ways.

Physical.Operating.Financial.Human.

A building is never only a building.

Finance and investing

I like markets for the same reason I like land.

They punish stories that do not survive the numbers.

  1. What changed?
  2. What is priced in?
  3. What is the base rate?
  4. What does history say?
  5. What assumption explains the current price?
  6. What would make the thesis wrong?
  7. Where is capital moving?
  8. How does regulation change the opportunity?
  9. How does infrastructure affect value?
  10. How do population, income and migration change demand?

I am increasingly interested in investing across different markets: Colombia first, and the growth markets of South America around it.

I read them the way I read a county before a project: population, income, migration, infrastructure, regulation, the currency, and what the price already assumes.

I am not a fund manager. I learn a market by studying it, and by taking part in it.

I study markets, and I take part in them.

Statistical thinking

Distributions.Base rates.Historical ranges.Probability.Absorption.

Numbers help me test whether the story I am telling myself survives reality.

A number becomes useful when I know what it is being compared with.

Architecture and construction

Materials.Sections.Joints.The order things were built in.

Construction eventually makes every idea answer to reality.

Structure is where imagination begins negotiating with gravity.

Languages

I learn languages to reach a culture, not a vocabulary.

What people say directly.What they soften.The joke that does not survive translation.The song that opens once the words do.

A culture changes when it stops arriving through translation.

Drawing

I draw to look.

A stem does not bend where memory expects it to.A leaf is rarely symmetrical.An orchid root goes where it wants.

Drawing punishes assumptions.

Code and systems

If I can describe the system clearly enough, I increasingly want to build it.

A scheduled check.A small tool that moves information.The way a paper airplane turns a chapter on this site.

How I use AI to build it lives in Inside My Mind.

Reading

I read slowly, and I read the same pages more than once.

Kenkō.Krogerus.A county ordinance.A title commitment.

The ordinance and the essay ask for the same attention.

A book I finish quickly is usually one I did not need.

Writing

I write to find out whether I understood it.

A sentence that will not come out straight is usually a thought that has not finished.

A memo.An entry.A note I never send.

Everything here is a notebook. I am in no hurry to call it anything else.

Everything I practise is an outline around something that won’t hold still.

I learn languages badly, and then less badly.
