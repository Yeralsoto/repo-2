Follow the Line

  1. What can this land become?

  2. Before the road, there is only possibility.

  3. Then someone draws a line.

  4. Every line changes the answer.

  5. An idea becomes a sequence.

  6. A place is more than its parcels.

  7. Eventually, the drawing disappears.

  8. People simply call it home.

  9. I tend to follow the line.
