A contract can change hands without the project staying the same

On paper, the agreement can look almost identical.

An original party. A contract. A seller. Then a new party enters.

Broadly, an assignment passes along rights under the contract, and the original party may still be responsible. A novation replaces a party altogether, and it needs everyone’s consent.

The paper may look similar. The people, the responsibilities and the risk around it have changed.

Who consented, and to what?
What moved: rights, obligations, or both?
Who is responsible if something goes wrong?
Did the economics change?
What should counsel, title and the other transaction professionals review?

Specific legal treatment depends on the agreement and the jurisdiction. Not legal advice.
