Made of concrete

There was a stretch when everything arrived at once. Tight deadlines. Incomplete information. Decisions that could not wait for clarity.

Some people froze and waited for certainty. Others made the call with what they had.

I did not know which one I was until there was no choice.

I build teams differently now. I notice who shows up when things get hard, not who sounds best when they are easy.

Pressure does not create who we are. It shows it.
