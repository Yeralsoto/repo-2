Work

IV — What I actually do

I underwrite land, and I decide what it can become.

Development strategy from first screen to closing: valuation, title, entitlement paths, deal structuring, and project management on live subdivisions. Most of my work is finding the reason a deal doesn't work before anyone has spent money finding out the hard way.

I work across more than ten US states, from Medellín.

About

V — The rest of it

I am trying to build a life I want to look at twice.

I read. I learn languages badly and then less badly. I drive out of the city most weekends because the light there does something the city can't. I am interested in where sensitivity comes from, and in what it takes to make peace with your own mind while you are also trying to build something.

The work pays for the life. The life is the point.
